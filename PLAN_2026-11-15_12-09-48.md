## 2. Score method

1. Each specification item gets one score from 0.0 to 1.0.
2. A score of 1.0 means that the code is built and a test passes.
3. A score of 0.5 means that the source is written but no compiler or test ran it.
4. A score of 0.0 means that no code exists.
5. A partial score means that some sub-items are built and some are not.
6. The score of one file is the mean of its item scores.
7. The total is the mean of the three file scores, weighted by line count.
